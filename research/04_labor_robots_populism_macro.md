# Fact Sheet 04 — AI/Robots Replacing Labor, Value Creation, Government Dependence, Populism, Macro Anchors

Compiled 2026-09-04 from web research. Facts only.

## 1. AI labor impact (2025–2026)

- Stanford "Canaries" Aug-2026 update: employment of 22–25-year-olds in AI-exposed occupations 19% below counterfactual (from ~13% in Aug-2025); effect runs through reduced hiring, concentrated where AI automates. — https://digitaleconomy.stanford.edu/news/canariesaug26/
- NY Fed: recent-grad unemployment 5.6% (H1 2026) vs 3.6% Mar-2019; underemployment 42%; grads more likely unemployed than overall workforce for first time since 1990. — https://www.newyorkfed.org/research/college-labor-market
- Goldman Sachs: AI cut US monthly payroll growth by ~16k net (Apr 2026), revised to ~11k/month (Jun 2026); +0.1pp unemployment. 300M jobs globally exposed; 6–7% US workforce displacement. — https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/19/goldman-ai-impact-employment-jobs.html ; https://www.goldmansachs.com/insights/articles/how-will-ai-affect-the-us-labor-market
- AI-cited layoffs: Amazon ~16,000 corporate roles Q1-2026; Salesforce support 9,000→5,000; Microsoft 4,800 Jul-2026; Accenture ~11,000. 50% of 2026 layoff events cite AI — 172,044 workers across 183 companies. — https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/25/the-running-list-major-tech-layoffs-in-2026-where-employers-cited-ai/
- US labor market: July 2026 payrolls −23k; unemployment 4.1%. — https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/03/august-2026-jobs-report-payrolls.html
- Anthropic Economic Index (Mar 2026): productivity estimate 1.8pp/yr headline → ~1.0pp adjusted → 0.6–0.8pp with bottlenecks. — https://www.anthropic.com/research/economic-index-march-2026-report
- Korea — Bank of Korea (Aug 2026): youth (15–34) jobs fell 285k Jun-2022→Jun-2026; 94% of decline in high-AI-exposure industries. — https://en.sedaily.com/international/2026/08/18/bok-warns-ai-threatens-college-graduates-jobs-first
- Korea — KDI (Jul 2026): AI to reduce jobs by 256,000 over 10 years (2.1% of 2024 employment). — https://www.kdi.re.kr/eng/research/reportView?pub_no=18370
- Korea youth labor (Jul 2026): youth employment rate 44.2% (−1.6pp YoY), 26th straight monthly decline; youth unemployment 6.8%; overall 2.6%. — https://en.sedaily.com/finance/2026/08/12/korea-adds-108000-jobs-in-july-rebounding-above-100000
