# Fact Sheet 05 — M&A Trends: Large-cap, Mid-market, Startups, Korea

Compiled 2026-09-04 from web research. Facts only.

## 1. Global M&A volume/value

- 2025 FY: $4.6T deal value, +49% YoY, highest since 2021; 68 megadeals >$10B (most since 1980); deal count −7% to lowest since 2016 (LSEG). Bain counts $4.8T; deals >$5B = 75% of strategic deal-value growth. — https://www.pymnts.com/acquisitions/2025/worldwide-m-and-a-value-leapt-50percent-in-2025-driven-by-looser-us-regulations/ ; https://www.bain.com/about/media-center/press-releases/20252/global-ma-stages-great-rebound-in-2025-with-$4.8-trillion-deal-value-to-mark-second-highest-total-on-record
- H1 2026 (LSEG): $2.8T announced, +48% YoY, strongest H1 since 1980; 47 deals >$10B (>$1.3T); ~24,000 deals, −9% YoY, weakest H1 count in six years; Technology $649B; Europe +88%; US 63% of global value. — https://www.lseg.com/en/insights/data-analytics/separating-the-signal-from-the-noise-m-and-a-booms-in-early-2026
- Bain midyear (Jun 2026): Jan–May value $2.4T (+41%); stock-plus-cash at record 35% of megadeal mix. — https://www.bain.com/about/media-center/press-releases/2026/global-ma-momentum-builds-in-2026-as-megadeals-surge-but-acquirers-confront-a-new-ai-winners-paradoxbain-company-ma-midyear-report/
- PwC midyear (Jun 2026): 2026 volumes on track −12%, values −2%; US megadeals 64% of US value. — https://www.pwc.com/gx/en/services/deals/trends.html
